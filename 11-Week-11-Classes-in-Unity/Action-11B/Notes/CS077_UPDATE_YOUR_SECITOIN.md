## Whiteboard
Storytelling
People: Kevin, Luisa, Pascual, Roman, Vinh
We did a 10 minute sessions where we created and imagined our story. We opted from the Void Runner story and decided to do our own story. The story we created is Pilot Cat. A 1930's era criminal cat from Catlumbia on the run in a world called Cat World. He has been on the run for years and is the number one Cat Nip dealer in the world. He has been flying over the Catlantic for years smuggling Cat Nip into Catmerica. Pilot Cat loves to sleep, fly and his precious Cat Nip. His lifestyle is not a legal one and he is currently being hunted by the International Cat Police. As Pilot Cat always says, "Meow!"
